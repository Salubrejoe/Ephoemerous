## Description  (≤4000)
The night sky above you, drawn true. Every star, planet, and constellation sits exactly where it stands over your spot on Earth, this very minute — no guesswork, no generic sky.

TWO WAYS TO SEE
Your Horizon frames the dome overhead, the way you'd actually look up from where you stand. One gesture rolls it into the Celestial view — the whole sphere seen from the pole, the way astronomers and centuries of astrolabes have drawn it. The change is a single continuous morph, not a cut.

WIND THE HEAVENS
Turn time by hours, days, months, or years and watch the sky answer: the Moon fills and empties, the planets wander the zodiac, the Sun climbs and sets. The sky has always been a clock — here you hold the hands.

TAP TO KNOW, REMEMBER TO KEEP
Touch anything to learn it — the Moon's phase and rise, a planet's bearing, a star's coordinates. Found something you love? Remember it. It stays marked on your sky from then on, and kept in step across your devices.

ANYWHERE ON EARTH
Northern nights or southern, your street or the open sea — the sky is honest everywhere. Travel the map to stand under another latitude entirely and see what rises there.

CARRY IT WITH YOU
Pin any object to a Home Screen widget that tracks it live. Or wear the Orloj — a true working astrolabe modelled on Prague's 600-year-old astronomical clock — on your Apple Watch, and wind the whole sky through time with the Digital Crown.

No accounts. No ads. Location is used only to draw your true horizon, and never leaves your device.

Look up. Remember.
